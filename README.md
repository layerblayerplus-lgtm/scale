- ## how the scale works
- you can pick up and drop items by clicking them
- you can place items on the scale
- diffrent items have different weights
- the scale will show you which is item is heavier or if they are equal weight
- if you want to weigh multiple items at once drop one item onto the other and then pick them both up
- there is a reset button that will ungroup any item on the desk
- ## how the game works
- there is a sign that tells you what the weight of the ironbar and feather
- you have to use the scale with the other three items to find the weight of the gold
- you have this counter to geuss the weight of the gold
- when you think the weight is right you click the button on the counter to lock it in
- you also have a sanity level the more times you mess up the weight you go more insane
- also there are ghosts
- ### the four items so far are:
- feather
- rock
- iron bar
- gold bar
